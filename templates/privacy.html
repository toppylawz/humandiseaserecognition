Privacy, terms and medical disclaimer

In short: your photo is never stored, a result is not a diagnosis, and anything that worries you belongs with a professional.

Privacy

  1. Your photo is sent over an encrypted connection, analysed in memory and deleted straight away. It is never saved and never used for training.
  2. The camera switches on only when you press Start camera, and only on the analyse page.
  3. The server keeps standard logs, such as the time of a request and the browser type, only to keep the service secure.
  4. One session cookie is needed for the site to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.
  5. Messages sent through the contact form are kept only to reply to you.

Terms of use

  1. Dermopsis is free to use for learning and research.
  2. Upload only photos you have the right to use, and never photos of someone else without their consent.
  3. Do not try to overload, copy or misuse the service.
  4. The model, the site and its content belong to Avotrax Limited. The research behind it is part of an MSc project at Sheffield Hallam University.

Medical disclaimer

Dermopsis is not a medical device and does not diagnose anything.

  1. Its first answer is right about 6 times in 10. The right condition is in its top five about 9 times in 10.
  2. It has not yet been tested separately across skin tones, so it may be less reliable for some.
  3. It knows 34 conditions and healthy skin. It cannot recognise anything outside them.
  4. A heatmap shows what influenced the model, not where a disease is.
  5. Call 999 or go to A&E for a rash that spreads fast with a fever, or a severe allergic reaction. See a GP soon about a mole that changes, or a sore that will not heal. Call NHS 111 if you are unsure.
  6. Using Dermopsis does not create a relationship with any doctor or healthcare provider.

Fairness and transparency

  1. We are looking for partners to test it across a wider range of skin tones.
  2. Every result shows its explanation and the cover test, so you can judge it rather than take it on trust.

Changes to these notes
