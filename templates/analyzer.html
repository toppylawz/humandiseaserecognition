Analyse a skin photo

Use a sharp close-up in daylight. The photo is analysed in memory and deleted straight away.

Hold the camera about 10 cm from the skin and fill the corners.

For a useful result

  • Fill most of the frame with the affected skin
  • Avoid shadows, glare, filters and zoom
  • Keep backgrounds plain: the model can notice them

    This is not a diagnosis. If a mark is new, changing, bleeding or painful, see a GP or pharmacist. Call 999 for a rash that spreads fast with a fever.
